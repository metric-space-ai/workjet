# ctox.dev device keys

Workjet registers a device key after a fresh ctox.dev sign-in. It can use this
key to restore your managed instances when the account session expires or
local encrypted session storage becomes unavailable.

Settings → Konto · ctox.dev shows when the key was created, last used and when
it expires. The ellipsis menu's **Neu einrichten** action registers a
replacement using a fresh ctox.dev session. Enrollment limits and sign-in
requirements appear in the same row.

Keys expire after thirty days without use and at most 180 days after creation.
If a key expires or is revoked, sign in normally to register a new one. Signing
out keeps Workjet signed out until you sign in again.

Manage or revoke your registered devices at ctox.dev's device management page.
Recovery grants access only to the instances included when the key was enrolled
and still permitted by your current membership.
