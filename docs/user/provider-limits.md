# Provider limits

Settings → Models shows the remaining allowance for each reported account window.
100% means unused; 0% means exhausted. The bar shrinks toward zero, turns yellow
at 20% remaining and red at 5%. “Limit reached” appears only when the reported
remaining allowance is exactly zero.

Short windows are labelled “Short-term”; their duration is in the tooltip.
Longer windows show “7 Tage” or “30 Tage” when the provider identifies that period.
The tooltip includes the observation time and the next reported reset.

An ambiguous, missing or expired reading shows “unbekannt”. A rejected request
alone does not establish an exhausted allowance. Accounts without reported
limits show a dash. These rules also apply to provider accounts on your instance.
